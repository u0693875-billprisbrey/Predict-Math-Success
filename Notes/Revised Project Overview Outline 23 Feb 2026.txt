CAN THE EARLY INTERVENTION BE A RECOMMENDATION TO TAKE A DIFFERENT COURSE?  WHAT IMPACT WOULD THAT HAVE ON THE GRADE? 
  --> This is a more complicated question, as there are different predictors of course selection than grade performance.
      It appears that ACT Math test scores are the best predictor of course selection.  However, that accuracy disappeared in the last couple of years, while grades increased.  So it's a more complicated, dynamic picture that may be reflecting shifting expectations.
	
	POSSIBILITY #1:  COURSE SELECTION DOESN'T MATTER.   These courses are all leveled as appropriate for an incoming freshman.	
	POSSIBILITY #2:  COURSE SELECTION IS ALREADY OPTIMAL.  We can't find a course signal because the best course is already picked for a student between the prior information and the grade performance.  Whatever that black box is, it's working fine, so we don't see the mis-placed students that would give us a signal.	
	
  --> Statistical Test:  Matching.  Find students with identical incoming qualifications in different courses and compare their performance.
