**EXPLANATION:** 

This document is a demonstration of a report that could be used to help the incoming freshman select a math course.   It shows the actual performance of students from the year 2024 compared to their grade projections.  

It provides the statistical distance to the qualifications of successful students in the prior year's courses.  Academic performance roughly decreases with distance and courses with a distance measure larger than three are not recommended.  

It also provides individual grade expectations per course based on available information about the student and the department's historical records.  

These projected grades have a large margin of error and many students who are projected to perform well end up performing poorly, and vice versa.  Individual effort is important.  Qualities like motivation and factors like concurrent course load, course schedule, and individual circumstances are not captured in the projections.
